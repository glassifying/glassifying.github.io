BM CLIENT

Build and test tools for Blueberry Playground. It sits on top of the real game and only shows you information or works on your own builds. It doesn't change the rules, time, ranges, or anyone else's stuff.

Install

Your own downloaded copymain
If you saved Blueberry Playground as an .html file, pick it here and it opens with the client added. The file stays on your computer; nothing gets uploaded.
Multiplayer may not connect this way, because the game runs from your file instead of its own site.
Userscript
With Tampermonkey or Violentmonkey, install bmc.user.js and the client loads every time you open the game.

What it does

CheckpointsTrack your build; it auto-saves when you add parts, not when it gets damaged. Respawn the last good copy.
Tournament checkFlags banned materials, the nuke, and edited health.
Damage numbersSee every hit and how hard.
Hover infoName, health bar, power, range and aim of whatever you point at.
Aim linesWhere your guns point. Your build only in multiplayer.
Wire viewBlue dot = input, orange = output, yellow line = powered.
Sensor beamsEach sensor's beam and range.
Jiggle meterHighlights parts of your build that spin or shake.
Center of massShows why a build tips over.
RulerPress \ twice to measure distance.
ReplayGhost replay of the last 5 seconds at quarter speed.
Lag meterFPS, physics time and body count.
Test rangeFrozen dummies and balloon planes. Single player only.

Fair play

A small "BM Client on" tag shows in the corner so nobody thinks you're hiding it. In multiplayer the test range is off and aim lines only show your own build. Blueberry Playground belongs to its developer; this page doesn't contain or share the game.
